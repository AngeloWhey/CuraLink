# Four-coax input impedance

**Release requires at least 10 MΩ from 300 Hz through 7 kHz at each protected electrode input.** The measurement plane is immediately after the local 47 kΩ electrode emulator. Coax capacitance falls on the generator side. Protection, coupling, copper and the amplifier input remain inside the measured impedance. D-003 and D-025 define this requirement.

The existing four-channel TLV9004 input and 22 MΩ DC bias reach only 2.962 MΩ at 7 kHz with 2 pF allocated to the trace and 0.5 pF to ESD protection. With an ideal quiet 22 MΩ shunt, a 10 MΩ magnitude at 7 kHz permits just 2.03 pF total parallel capacitance. TI specifies a typical 5 pF common-mode input capacitance for this amplifier. A low-impedance generator masks much of this loading. The complete circuit also drives shared bias nodes, so a shunt-capacitance calculation alone cannot qualify it. [TI TLV9004](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9004.pdf)

The positive-feedback study covers 41 cases on the original bias network and 19 on the alternate bias connection. Each LTspice run uses the four-channel fixture with four 100 pF cables, one quiet local reference and the original TI-family amplifier models. Results are driven-port ratios calculated from complex voltage and current. Other signal sources are AC-grounded through their stated source impedances. The 100 Hz–10 MHz AC traces check peaking, while the reported minimum and conductance use only 300 Hz–7 kHz. AC peaking and a negative real admittance alone do not establish oscillation.
