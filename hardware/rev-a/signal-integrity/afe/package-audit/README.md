# AFE package and land-pattern audit — 2026-10-02

The root-requested custom footprints are available in `hardware/rev-a/pcb/footprints/CuraLink.pretty/`. Actual KiCad 9.0.9 `pcbnew.FootprintLoad` parsed both files successfully. The validator checked every numbered copper pad's coordinate/size against the TI reference-land tables, the 0.05 mm mask expansion, the complete canonical physical pin sets and the quad supply / ESD ground assignments. Results and file hashes are in `native-parse-validation.json`.

| Custom footprint | Manufacturer reference | Lands and numbering | Paste / mask |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TLV_RUC14_X2QFN_2x2mm_NoEP` | TI RUC0014A, drawing 4220584/A (05/2019) | Fourteen 0.6 × 0.2 mm lands; five on each vertical edge at 0.4 mm pitch, two on top and two on bottom. Side centerline spacing 1.8 mm, top/bottom centerline spacing 1.8 mm; top/bottom pair spacing 0.4 mm. **No exposed pad.** Pin 1 is the upper-left side land in top view. | Same-sized paste apertures; 0.05 mm corner radius; NSMD opening expansion 0.05 mm. Chosen 3 × 3 mm courtyard includes pad/body clearance. |
| `TPD4E05U06DQA_USON10_1x2.5mm` | TI DQA0010A, drawing 4220328/A (12/2015) | Ten physical lands at 0.5 mm pitch and x = ±0.4175 mm. All are 0.565 mm long. Signals and mechanical NC lands are 0.2 mm wide; GND pins 3 and 8 are 0.4 mm wide. **No exposed-pad number.** Pin 1 upper-left; NC pins 6, 7, 9 and 10 retained. | Signal paste matches copper; two **unnumbered F.Paste-only** apertures at GND 3/8 are 0.565 × 0.36 mm per the stencil example. NSMD expansion 0.05 mm; chosen 1.9 × 3.1 mm courtyard. |

The separate paste-only pads have no electrical nets or copper. Integration must skip pads with an empty number when assigning canonical nets. They are stencil apertures, not extra package pins. The package's original pads 3 and 8 still carry GND. The 0.05 mm mask expansions leave a nominal minimum 0.10 mm mask web between the closest neighboring apertures; the assembler must qualify the actual mask registration and stencil process.

The quad amplifier RUC physical pin map is **14 OUT_A, 1 IN_A−, 2 IN_A+, 6 OUT_B, 5 IN_B−, 4 IN_B+, 7 OUT_C, 8 IN_C−, 9 IN_C+, 13 OUT_D, 12 IN_D−, 11 IN_D+, 3 V+, 10 V−**. It matches the prior manufacturer audit and the canonical TLV9004IRUCR/TLV9054IRUCR nine-package maps. The DQA map is **1 D1+, 2 D1−, 3 GND, 4 D2+, 5 D2−, 6 NC, 7 NC, 8 GND, 9 NC, 10 NC**, checked against TI Figure 4-3 / Table 4-2. Canonical NC pads remain unconnected; TI permits them to float or be grounded, but that option is not changed here.

TI documents two DQA ground-metal variants under TPD4E05U06DQAR: A has separate center ground lands and B joins the center ground metal. A [TI applications engineer confirms](https://e2e.ti.com/support/interface-group/interface/f/interface-forum/1384139/tpd4e05u06-what-is-the-correct-package-drawing) that either variant can ship and either land pattern is compatible. The custom footprint deliberately uses the A reference land and stencil; no unfilled via is added inside a paste aperture. Place external nearby ground vias for the ESD return.

The official KiCad library was inspected rather than assumed correct for the selected suffix. `Package_DFN_QFN:Texas_S-PX2QFN-14` has the correct RUC pad order and no EP, but its IPC-generated 0.875 × 0.2 mm copper lands differ from TI's 0.6 × 0.2 mm reference lands. This audit does not declare that IPC geometry wrong; the custom file deliberately follows the manufacturer's reference. `Package_SON:USON-10_2.5x1.0mm_P0.5mm` is sourced from a different part, TPD4E02B04, with equal 0.55 × 0.30 mm lands and ±0.385 mm centers; it does not reproduce the selected TPD4E05U06's wider GND lands. That generic file was not copied into this project.

Primary source PDFs are preserved under `hardware/rev-a/ltspice/sources/`: [TLV9004](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9004.pdf) reference drawings are PDF pages 107–109, [TLV9054](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9054.pdf) pages 85–87, and [TPD4E05U06](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd4e05u06.pdf) DQA A pages 28–30 / DQA B pages 31–33. `footprint-audit.json` preserves primary PDF hashes and complete coordinate tables. The source drawings, generated pad-number previews and native KiCad geometry were visually inspected. This is a mechanical/pin/land audit; it does not qualify solder volume, stencil thickness, reflow, complete board DRC or the assembler's exact land-pattern acceptance.

Reproduction: run `build_footprints.py` with the bundled Windows Python to recreate the two custom files and `footprint-audit.json`; run `validate_footprints.py` with KiCad's WSL system Python. The independent two-footprint `footprint-validation.kicad_pcb` is a parser-validation fixture only, not the CuraLink system board.
