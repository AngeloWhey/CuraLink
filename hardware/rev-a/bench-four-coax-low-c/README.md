## Changed signal path

- U631–U634 are individual ADI MAX44280AXT+T preamps for CH1–4. U635 serves the shared REF. Each uses the existing 10 kΩ feedback and 1 kΩ return for noninverting gain 11. The part requires a minimum stable gain of 5. Its 1.8–5.5 V range covers the switched 3 V analog supply and the 1.5 V midpoint. [ADI datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX44280.pdf)
- Each SC70-6 has IN+ at pad 1, GND at 2, IN− at 3, OUT at 4, active-low shutdown at 5 and VDD at 6. Tie 5 and 6 to AVDD. The analog rail switch controls the whole bank. Place C1059 at U631 and C632–C635 at U632–U635 respectively. All five are 100 nF. [ADI pin descriptions](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX44280.pdf)
- Retire the former U101 quad. Keep U109B as the original unity-stable VMID driver. U109A/C/D are local closed-loop followers on VMID_RAW. This preserves every physical amplifier in the powered quad. The native XML reports all 14 pins.
- Return R1002/R1020/R1038/R1056/R1146, each 22 MΩ, to **VMID_RAW**, the 10 kΩ/10 kΩ divider bypassed with 2.2 µF. Other audio feedback elements stay on VMID beyond the existing 47 Ω isolator. The 22 MΩ returns carry negligible average signal current into the filtered divider. Moving them avoids feedback from shared load current through the high-impedance input network. Full frequency and offset results are in the [vendor-model simulation record](../input-impedance/max44280/README.md).

## Placement and acceptance

The 47 kΩ source resistors belong at the AFE end of each generator feed. Keep the resistor outputs, ESD escape, 5.1 kΩ limiter, coupling-capacitor nodes and preamp pin 1 compact. Keep amplified outputs, clocks, RF and charger copper away. Count pads and test access in the added capacitance. **Target at most 0.60 pF total added board capacitance per protected input**, over and above the device input and ESD models. The 0.75 pF stress case leaves little margin. The PCB copy and DRC may establish geometric intent. Only extracted or measured loading can establish the actual capacitance.

Measure 300 Hz–7 kHz impedance at the protected input plane just after the 47 kΩ, using calibrated attenuation or complex voltage/current, identical leads and an open-fixture baseline. A normal passive oscilloscope probe can alone exceed the capacitance allowance. Check all four channels independently. REF is tied to local ground through R605 in this bench build. To excite REF, isolate its grounded end and restore it after the test. Exercise positive/negative offset recovery, simultaneous aggressors, actual input noise and 3 V rail draw. Verify full-stage stability with the actual feedback and bypass layout. The five new amplifiers alone draw 3.75 mA typical and up to 6 mA using the datasheet's 0.75/1.2 mA per enabled device. [ADI electrical characteristics](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX44280.pdf)
