**RUN voltage headroom.** For TI's 3 V TPS7A20 accuracy test condition at 3.3 V input, the loss budget down to nominal 3.50 V pack cutoff is only 0.20 V for D610, two PMOSs, U511, interconnect, pack sag and ripple together. At the earlier lower cutoff corner of 3.35 V it is 0.05 V. At a 3.80 V restart it is 0.50 V. These differences are available voltage budgets. Actual diode drop depends on current and temperature. If U513 sags below the intended supply tolerance, the comparator's previously calculated approximately 1.53 V rising digital-good threshold is too low to certify a 3 V MCU/ADC rail. Sweep pack emulator voltage at peak radio current, log VBAT, SYS_RAW, SYS_DIGITAL_IN, DVDD/VDDA and resets together, then update the threshold/path or operating limit if needed. [TI TPS7A20 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a20.pdf) · [Underlying cutoff calculations](../power/power-design.md)

**Weak-field reporting.** The comparator estimates 2.62 V rising / 2.25 V falling at WPT_RAW under the documented nominal assumptions. It can inhibit RUN while CHG_IN, after input protection drops, is too low to support a 3 V MCU rail through D611. With the pack path then open the radio can go silent or reset. Upon removal, sufficient pack voltage and the RUN delay can restore the rail. Slow field ramps, misalignment near threshold, charge completion and repeated load steps must be tested on an oscilloscope. The laptop should show stale/disconnected status with a last-received timestamp during a blackout. Continuous telemetry through this region would require changes to the digital power path or hysteretic control plus verification.

**External-pack fallback.** J501 accepts a compatible **protected** pack or current-limited battery emulator with confirmed polarity, voltage and NTC interface. Disconnect the receiver with R542 open and disconnect the bench charge source. For a pack charged only externally, leave R501 open to isolate BAT_CHG and retain R502 for battery RUN. In the four-channel starting BOM J502, R501 and R542 are DNP; R502 and bench-input R543 are fitted. Confirm those links physically for each bring-up configuration. Treat battery limits, fixture isolation and unknown lab cell identification separately from this schematic check.
