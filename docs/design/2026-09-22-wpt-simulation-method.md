# CuraLink WPT simulation method

**2026-09-22 · Selected workflow DEC-WPT-002 revised by DEC-WPT-003 / handbook D-017 and D-018.** This is a method specification, not a completed simulation or proof of charging performance. Rev A reuses the 13.56 MHz Luo et al. planar coil pair—40 mm square TX, 10 mm square RX and 10 mm nominal separation—and starts in ADS from its measured coupled-inductor parameters. HFSS → Touchstone `.s2p` remains the required physical-model path if geometry, materials, separation or nearby structures change materially. [Luo et al., 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10302922/).

## Model boundary and sequence

1. **ADS measured-coil baseline:** Model two coupled inductors with the paper's measured 13.56 MHz values: `L_TX = 2.49 µH`, series `R_TX = 1.80 Ω`, `L_RX = 0.330 µH`, series `R_RX = 0.399 Ω` and `k = 0.0662` (`M ≈ 60.0 nH`). This represents the published 40 mm / 10 mm coils at 10 mm nominal coaxial separation. Do not import the paper's matching capacitors or its 50 Ω transformed load. Follow the concrete [ADS schematic setup](2026-09-22-ads-wpt-model-setup.md); no public original Touchstone file was found.
2. **ADS CuraLink first pass:** Add a defined RF source and source impedance, newly designed resonant/matching network, rectifier model including package parasitics, reservoir capacitor and a resistor as the first DC load. Sweep source power, load, tuning tolerance and k around the nominal value as a sensitivity study; under D-019, label k sweeps as assumed because CuraLink has no VNA measurement. Use harmonic balance for the nonlinear rectifier and check convergence versus harmonic order. [Keysight harmonic-balance guide](https://edadownload.software.keysight.com/eedl/ads/2011/pdf/adshbapp.pdf), [Keysight wireless rectifier case study](https://www.keysight.com/in/en/assets/7018-05801/case-studies/5992-2395.pdf).
3. **ADS later pass:** Replace the resistor with candidate receiver regulation/OVP, charger input behavior and charge-state-dependent loading. Inspect no-load/full-battery voltage, weak coupling, maximum coupling, rectifier/device dissipation and startup/transients. Harmonic balance alone does not validate charger control, transient mode switching, cell safety or thermal rise; use appropriate transient/thermal models and hardware measurements separately.
4. **Conditional HFSS path:** If the published geometry/materials/separation are not reproduced, or nearby copper, battery, ferrite or enclosure materially perturbs the link, model the physical pair in HFSS and export a passive two-port `.s2p` for ADS. Record geometry, materials, ports, sweep and mesh convergence; do not place a tuning element in both tools. [Ansys network-data export documentation](https://ansyshelp.ansys.com/public/Views/Secured/Electronics/v251/en/Subsystems/HFSS/Subsystems/HFSS%20Scripting/Content/ExportNetworkDataPlanarEM.htm?TocPath=Design+Object+Script+Commands%7C_____46), [Ansys renormalization notes](https://ansyshelp.ansys.com/public/Views/Secured/Electronics/v261/en/Subsystems/HFSS3DLayout/Content/ReportsandPostProc/ExportingMatrixData.htm).

## Primary result and comparison rules

For the initial resistive DC load, report `V_DC` measured across `R_LOAD` and `P_DC = V_DC²/R_LOAD` from the **DC component** of load voltage. Report DC ripple separately. Define the RF-to-DC efficiency as `η_RF→DC = P_DC / P_RF,accepted at the TX coil-model input port`; record the ADS source power and port/reference plane so accepted RF power is reproducible. If comparing rectifier-only efficiency, use *accepted RF power at the rectifier input* and name it separately. Later report TX-supply-DC-to-load-DC efficiency with transmitter losses included, not under the RF-to-DC label.

The optimization ranking is **delivered DC power at useful voltage and RF-to-DC efficiency** over the required geometry/load envelope. L, R, Q, M, k, SRF and S21 are diagnostic explanations, not success metrics on their own. A high unloaded Q or favorable S21 can coexist with inadequate rectified power after nonlinear loading.

For correlation, the paper also reports `Q_TX = 118` and `Q_RX = 70.4` at the nominal geometry. Its reported 70% uses `S21 = -1.55 dB` after matching between 50 Ω VNA ports; it is coil-link RF efficiency, not rectified-load efficiency. Evaluate the rectifier and CuraLink load separately.

## Minimum reproducible record

Retain the coil-model source and values; ADS schematic/version, source power/impedance, tuning/rectifier models and parameter sweeps; load resistance, DC voltage/ripple, estimated accepted RF power, DC power, efficiency, component peak voltage/current and dissipations. Under D-019, retain end-to-end test conditions and results at nominal, weak and strong coupling, offset/misaligned cases, and more than one useful load; do not claim measured L/R/Q, k or S-parameters. If HFSS is reopened, also retain the model/revision, materials, port planes, mesh convergence, `.s2p` file and reference impedance; verify passivity and port ordering. No numerical CuraLink performance is claimed until simulation or end-to-end measurement exists.
