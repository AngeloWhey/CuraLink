What to build
-------------
1. Electronics design for layout:
   hardware/rev-a/bench-four-coax-low-c/curalink-rev-a.kicad_pro
   This candidate includes five MAX44280 gain-11 input stages. Its separate
   80 x 74 mm placement contains 334 footprints, 499 unrouted connections
   and two antenna-edge findings. The four-coax TLV baseline is retained in
   hardware/rev-a/bench-four-coax for comparison. The parent board has eight
   channels and older power wiring. The low-C folder has a printable PDF.
2. Generator-driven TX: hardware/wpt/bench-rf/tx/tx-matching.kicad_pro
   Receiver: hardware/wpt/bench-rf/rx/rx-matching.kicad_pro
   The two RF sheets terminate at the published spiral lands. Preserve Luo's
   spiral geometry, approximately 0.8 mm FR-4, 1 oz copper and 10 mm nominal
   spacing. Provide trim access. A nearby cell, plane or metal holder can
   detune the link. Keep the TX return separate from receiver DC ground.
   The selectable ADS schematic is MyLibrary_lib:CuraLink_RX_BAS4002A_HB
   in ads/MyWorkspace_wrk. The four-pin package opens as BAS4002A_SOT143.
3. Firmware and host: four indexed channels in RECORD; charger status only
   in CHARGE. The same laptop receives BLE in both tests. Implement these
   against the GPIO names and state table below. Validate with actual packets.

Test A | Four coax inputs, wireless charging disconnected
--------------------------------------------------------
Use a current-limited battery emulator on the protected pack input J501.
Begin at 4.0 V after checking the harness pinout. For signal-only testing
leave the charger-to-pack link R501 open; R502 enables the protected RUN
branch. Leave the wireless and external 5 V sources disconnected. A real
externally charged 1S pack can replace the emulator after its identity,
protection, polarity and discharge limits are verified. Use its compatible
external charger with the pack disconnected from CuraLink.

Each U.FL center feeds one channel. Return shields to board ground. Put the
47 kΩ emulator near the AFE after any long low-impedance feed trace. Keep the
shared REF on its own matched AC-coupled quiet-ground path. Calibrate generator
amplitude after any external attenuator using the intended termination. Keep
the generator offset at zero initially. A magnitude shown for a matched 50 Ω
load can double on a high-impedance input. Keep an RF charging generator
separate from the microvolt signal inputs.

Start with one 200 µVpp, 1 kHz sine and the other three inputs held quiet.
Expect roughly 194 mVpp on the active ADC pad centered near 1.503 V in the
nominal model. Repeat on every connector, including the last lane. Then test
three coherent aggressors, four separate input patterns, DC offset recovery,
startup blanking, channel ordering, noise, ADC settling and uninterrupted BLE.
One generator passively split four ways cannot establish channel independence.
Record the actual cable capacitance, attenuator impedance and AFE input plane.
The complete AFE must present at least 10 MΩ over 300 Hz through 7 kHz.
Measure at the AFE side of the local 47 kΩ emulator with protection and bias
installed. The TLV baseline models 2.96 MΩ at 7 kHz. The low-C candidate
models at least 10.70 MΩ across the band in both ADS and LTspice with 0.60 pF
additional layout capacitance per input. At 0.75 pF it gives 10.08 MΩ. At
1.00 pF it gives only 9.16 MΩ. A 5.47 mm protected-net span already exists
before routing. Re-place parts if needed, extract the full capacitance
including pads and stubs, then measure the finished assembly. The five
high-value inputs return to VMID_RAW, a bypassed divider. Keep the other
gain-setting returns on VMID. The analog supply model draws 6.02 mA.

To measure impedance without a VNA, drive one channel at a time with WPT off.
Compare two known impedances placed at the board end, between the low-Z feed
and protected AFE. Keep the input tone and other three channel terminations
unchanged. Example total series values RA = 47 kΩ and RB = 1.047 MΩ (include
the fixed resistor). Capture the complex fundamental output at the same
low-impedance ADC pad in both cases, referenced to the generator. With
q = output_A/output_B, Zin = (RB - q*RA)/(q - 1). Use calibrated complex RA
and RB if fixture capacitance matters. Check the fixture first against known
10 MΩ and parallel-RC standards. A probe directly on the high-impedance
input can consume the entire capacitance budget. Sweep all four channels
from 300 Hz to 7 kHz and bound instrument, fixture and resistor uncertainty.

Test B | Wireless charging with live BLE
---------------------------------------
First power the protected 5 V interface from the current-limited bench input
using R543 and open R542. Use a battery emulator rated to sink the test charge
current. When switching to the receiver, open R543 before fitting R542.
Connect the receiver DC return and regulated WPT_RX_DC to J502. Keep
generator-grounded signal cables and debug leads disconnected for isolated
wireless-power measurements. Use a differential probe on floating RF nodes.

The TX generator drives the TX matching network at 13.56 MHz. Use a spacer
for the published 10 mm reference before moving the coils by hand. Record
distance and alignment. Verify the generator or amplifier can deliver the
stated available power into 50 Ω at the coil fixture. 25 dBm is 316 mW or
11.25 Vpp into matched 50 Ω. Power quoted at the generator can exceed what
arrives at the board. Bench acceptance needs a usable range of positions.

Bring up the receiver with a dummy load first. Start with low drive. Measure
rectifier reservoir RECT_DC, regulated WPT_RX_DC, protected CHG_IN, both
radio and analog rails, and signed current into the emulator. Test startup,
full demand, taper, termination, open load, short/fault, and lift-off. Radio
bursts count as load. Record delivered DC watts and the actual TX power.
Observe bridge, capacitor, regulator, TVS, RX winding and pack temperatures.
Limit drive and remove the field at idle if continuous thermal headroom is
unproven. A disconnected eFuse can make the upstream receiver more lightly
loaded. A pulse rating on a TVS gives no sustained-power guarantee.

ADS with the fitted-coil model at nominal coupling gives these minimum raw
reservoir voltages (24 / 29 / 39 mA total receiver demand). At 25 dBm:
7.31 / 5.79 / 2.91 V. At 26 dBm: 9.20 / 7.66 / 4.69 V. Use 6 V raw as
the regulator headroom screen. In the 26 dBm coupled RF startup transient,
early radio demand held WPT_RX_DC below regulation. It first crossed 4.75 V
at 0.980 ms after the load was reduced. Qualify power-good before enabling a
radio burst and check restart after a weak field. These are simulated cases.
After a staged startup, the ADS envelope at 26 dBm held 4.998 V at 24 mA
and 4.997 V at 29 mA. A load seeking 39 mA at 5 V settled at 4.669 V and
36.42 mA. At 27 dBm near open load the protection simulation predicts up to
123 mW continuous TVS dissipation in the tested low-knee case. Establish a
thermal limit before choosing a continuous generator power setting.

Historical 20 mA and 4.1 V settings are emulator examples only. Before
connecting a lab cell for charging, identify its manufacturer/model, rated
charge voltage, charge/discharge current, allowed temperatures, protection
and NTC details, connector and pin polarity. A two-pin connector alone cannot
identify a cell. Set ILIM/VSET, ISET and thermal limits from the exact cell
data. Confirm the cell can supply radio peaks and one hour of measured RUN.

BLE mode and status
-------------------
RECORD: protected battery RUN enables the AFE, MCU and BLE.
CHARGE: protected receiver DC can power the MCU and BLE; hardware disables
the AFE. Acquisition stays stopped at charge completion until field removal.
With a weak field the AFE can be disabled before sufficient energy reaches
the radio. On the host, expire old reports on disconnect and say "status
unavailable." Measure transfers without relying on a connection remaining up.
The LTspice handover test drops DVDD through 1.71 V about 6.3 ms after loss
of protected RF power with an assumed 86 ms RUN inhibit. Expect to reconnect.

Read BQ25185 STAT1/STAT2 together with detected receiver DC and charge enable.
In pin order STAT1, STAT2 (H = high from the MCU-domain pull-up):
  H L    charger reports charging
  L H    recoverable fault
  L L    latched fault
  H H    completed, asleep, or disabled; distinguish only with evidence
Without a battery the charger can alternate between charging and completed.
Expose ambiguous and stale states. These pins cannot report battery percent,
actual charge current, or the specific cause within a fault class. Use a
signed current measurement to prove charging. TI BQ25185 datasheet Table 6-2.
https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25185.pdf

Remaining release work
----------------------
- Check the release status in the handbook and variant BOM before ordering.
  Finish manual routing, schematic-to-board parity, planes/return paths, RF
  feed, antenna keepout, crosstalk review, DRC, footprint/orientation and
  assembly checks. Confirm land pattern and drill/mask/paste at the factory.
- Qualify AFE loading, shared bias, recovery, powered-off injection and ADC
  reference noise on the new powered digital rail. Simulated performance does
  not include routing, connector leakage, ADC switching or RF burst pickup.
- Qualify RF capacitor tolerance, RF current/loss and coil/TVS heating over
  power, load, misalignment and intended continuous operating time. Fit final
  trim values from measured DC delivery; use regulator headroom as well as
  voltage at the charger. Compare available power to the generator rating.
- Verify digital-rail dropout and restart with the added OR diode at minimum
  protected pack voltage and during radio bursts. Check unit-to-unit behavior,
  rail handover, AFE discharge, reverse leakage and lack of back-power.
- Qualify exact battery and pack protection, charge configuration, firmware,
  BLE status, total runtime and manufacturing cost. Sourcing snapshots do not
  reserve inventory. No production order has been placed.
